Backend Engineer, Payments Platform

Remote (US) · Full-time · Engineering

About the role

We are hiring a Backend Engineer to build and scale the REST APIs behind our payments product. You will own performance, write Python every day, and lead a small team of engineers.

What you will do

  • Design, build and maintain REST APIs that process millions of payments a month
  • Improve the performance and reliability of our core services
  • Lead a small team: plan the work, review code and mentor
  • Work with product and design to ship features quickly

What we are looking for

  • 4+ years of backend experience, with strong Python
  • Experience with PostgreSQL, caching and event streaming (Kafka)
